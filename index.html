First-Time Voter

Turning 18? Registering for the first time?

🎯 Who This Is For

You're 18+ (or turning 18 before Jan 1 of election year), an Indian citizen, and have never registered to vote before. You may feel overwhelmed — we make it simple.

🗺️ Your 5-Step Journey

1Check eligibility (18+, Indian citizen)
2Register online — Form 6 at voters.eci.gov.in
3Receive your EPIC (Voter ID) card in 30 days
4Locate your polling booth before election day
5Vote on EVM — we show you exactly how

Returning Voter

Already registered, want to stay updated?

🎯 Who This Is For

You've voted before but may have moved, changed your name, or just want to double-check your registration is current for the upcoming election.

🗺️ Your 5-Step Journey

1Verify your name on the electoral roll
2Update address or details (Form 8) if needed

4Understand Model Code of Conduct
5Vote confidently on election day

NRI / Overseas Indian

Living abroad, want to exercise your vote?

🎯 Who This Is For

You're an Indian citizen living abroad with a valid Indian passport. Over 1.3 crore Indians live overseas — most don't know they have the right to vote.

🗺️ Your 5-Step Journey

1Confirm NRI eligibility (Indian passport holder)
2Fill Form 6A at voters.eci.gov.in
3Submit passport copy + overseas address proof
4Travel to India on election day (mandatory)
5Vote at your registered constituency booth

✨ What You'll Get Inside

📋 Form 6A guide🛂 NRI rights explainer📄 Document checklist🗺️ Constituency info⚠️ No postal vote rule🤖 AI answers 24/7
